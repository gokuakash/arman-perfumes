Our Story

A counter of scents on Beach Road since 2016.

ARMAN PERFUMES began as a single glass counter on Parrys' second lane — and grew into the neighbourhood's most trusted perfume house. Every bottle on our shelves is authentic, tested and priced for Chennai.

We hand-pour premium attar in store, blend bespoke fragrances to your skin, and keep the classic houses in stock — from everyday mists to occasion-worthy gift sets.

No samples that fade in an hour. No oils that sting the wrist. Just scent that speaks first — and lasts.
