DESIGN DIRECTION — "ORGANIC" ANCHOR (frontend-design skill, github.com/Ilm-Alan/frontend-design)
- Deliberate rejection of the safe dark+gold "luxury" default in favour of the Organic anchor:
  the unexpected pairing for a perfume store. Sun-washed earth tones, tactile paper, serif-only voice.
- Surface: sand #E8DCC7 / oat #F1E9D4 (NOT cream, NOT dark). Ink: espresso #2B2118.
- Accents: terracotta #C66B3D, ochre #C08E3A, moss #606C38, sage #8B9D83.
- Typography: Fraunces ONLY (single Google family; the anchor restricts Fraunces to Organic).
- Texture: full-page SVG feTurbulence grain at 5% opacity; halftone dot tile in gallery.
- Structure: rounded corners 16-32px, soft warm shadows, 1px low-opacity ink borders.
- Motion: gentle ease cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), 300-500ms; breathing float on hero collage; rotating stamp.
- Differentiator (one memorable move): rotating circular stamp seal ("ARMAN PERFUMES · PARRYS · EST. CHENNAI")
  overlapping a two-shot editorial collage in the hero, plus a second stamp seal on the about image.
