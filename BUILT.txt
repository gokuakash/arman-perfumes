FIX (2026-08-19, commit 3f5cf75)
  Massive paper gap on live site: html/body used overflow-x:hidden, which
  creates a scroll container and BREAKS position:sticky in Chrome — the
  hero stage and story card scrolled away instead of pinning, leaving
  ~150vh + ~220vh of empty background between sections.
  Replaced overflow-x:hidden with overflow-x:clip on html+body (clips
  overflow WITHOUT creating a scroll container, preserving sticky).
  Verified: per-viewport pixel sweep on mobile shows content in every
  frame (only a 152px intentional band in the final section); sticky
  pins at top:0 at scroll 844 / 3000; no h-overflow @390px.
  Redeployed, live md5 == local md5 (330568bbec209dcd25b68f344ba41c69)
